All of my anxiety is trying to manage what other people are thinking about me, and trying to make favorable impressions. It's really the thing I need to put down.

You can think of it as needing therapy. But maybe you don't. You can understand that it's thinking, and then start to see it. And see through it. Like worrying about what people think.

I can worry, and I'm thinking and trying to make good impressions, and it fucks me all up.

Why? I don't know. Early traumas? I just don't believe it, because I don't have the evidence. Maybe. Maybe not. But maybe it doesn't matter if you can put it down and recognize it as thinking. Thinking just like thinking about what goes into brownies, or whether the floor is clean, or work stuff. It's the same stuff that comes up during meditation.

If there are people making great progress on their personal issues by understanding root causes, keep doing whatever. But if you're like me and you get lost in the maze... just recognize that it's generating thinking, and reactions to some of that thinking, that make us feel uncomfortable. It threatens the comfort of the I.

And when you're thinking you're not really in touch, and you're not at ease. You aren't relaxed.

The truth is... just relax. Just do it. And when you're sitting, just sit. Don't watch it all.

Checking is such a big part of practice: looking and waiting for our minds to do something, like settle or get to a destination. But meditation is always at the origin. It's the origin-destination problem!

We all have to confront our thinking about our inferiority.

In fact, we can really think of our thinking in total as the issue. And we can classify it and start to see how it works. When do we get distracted and disconnected from our body and environment? Keep coming back to a clear mind. Not in a fight with reality. Not arguing with the moment. And not trying to extract something from it.

Maybe it's about being friends with this experience. Not a fake warm feeling. A genuine welcoming.
