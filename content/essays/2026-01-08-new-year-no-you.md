It's a new year. What will we accomplish this year? What will we set our mind to, and how will we achieve it?

If you're reading this you probably already appreciate the value of meditation, Zen, don't-know, clarity, listening—whatever you want to call it. That experience of slowing down or just shifting your attention away from the incessant chatter and aggression of the mind that's all structured around this idea of a separate self, a me.

But where is this me? Have you seen it? Have you touched it? It's the "you" in the sentence "have you touched it" that we're talking about.

Trying to force yourself to experience selflessness is just nonsense. That's more thinking, more delusion, more distraction. But sitting quietly, looking, observing…that's how we start to naturally peel away the layers of delusion till we get to the core.

New year, new no you.
