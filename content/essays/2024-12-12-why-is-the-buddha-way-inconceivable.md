I scribbled this in my journal one night and thought I'd share. For context, the fourth Great Vow is "the Buddha Way is inconceivable, we vow to attain it."

"The Buddha mind is no mind and it's inconceivable because it's literally not conceiving. When you're not conceiving anything, you're just here.

You can never make meditation "happen" because the entire act of trying to make something happen is what meditation is not. [So what is meditation?] It's not trying to change anything in what's happening. The actual state of your mind or experience is irrelevant. When you're just sitting there like a flower, without the opinions, strategizing, tension, thinking, worrying, speculating, all that stuff…when you're just sitting there quietly, that's meditation. Any effort will ultimately undo that, and you can see why….it's just more strategizing.

I've come to a few conclusions about how to attain that mind, the most recent of which is just waiting in a place and breathing. Maybe breathing comfortably. Then just waiting. That activity, after some time, tends to regulate the body-mind to where the mind is no longer producing thoughts and leading you into thought-world."
