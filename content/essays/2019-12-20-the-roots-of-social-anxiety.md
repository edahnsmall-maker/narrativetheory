Status projection is the basis of social anxiety. It's not narrative, it's the conclusion of narrative. It's ultimately thinking that's clouding empathy. We aren't seeing how people see themselves accurately: like outsiders, with their own suffering and loneliness. Like a hair stylist who is chatty but still shy, until you open them up and address the diffused feeling of exclusion people feel in the workplace.

People develop status ideas about themselves, but it's in response to information they're also receiving from others about their own personal self-worth. Not explicitly with words, but with clothing, posture, vocal tone, eye gaze, and lack of hesitation. Communication through body language. It's silent dialogue.

What exactly is maturity? Maturity is just intellectual insight and understanding. It's perspective that comes from having a clear understanding of something, and not being deluded by your own thoughts about something and how it serves your needs.
