Not being yourself is the essential problem of our entire generation. It's why people start pretending to be other people. And it's the reason why people fight.

You learn how to be yourself by not attacking yourself, and just relaxing and just being who you are. Stop thinking about it, stop worrying about it, stop analyzing it... just be yourself. Don't feel embarrassed. Don't attack yourself.

We don't know how to be ourselves because we put conditions on what that's supposed to look like and how that's supposed to be received. And how it's supposed to affect others. And we think a lot about how other people are going to feel when they see us, and when they interact with us. And then we start behaving in a way that we think we should. It's thinking and behaving, and it separates the action and the moment from the experience of it. It adds the layer of Saran Wrap onto things, and it's not just Saran Wrap that is the result of thinking. It's also what you end up doing with your body, and maybe how you hold it, and how you relax it and how you don't.

So the big idea here is that we learn that we can't just be ourselves. We can't be natural, we can't relax. That's Taoism. And because of that we start really falling apart and suffering. And it's hard to just simply relax. That's why Alan Watts probably called it the age of anxiety.

When our minds are active and being dragged and pulled, they form conceptions of things and they form judgments upon things, and that judgment leads to thinking and resistance. A judgment world leads to a world where we're trying to control and trying to monitor. I'm not talking about having no interaction with things. I'm talking about trying to manage and worry and think about the future, and having a goal, an objective. And anytime we have that goal and objective, things start to go wrong. Our body gets tense. We start to manage the way that we appear. We stop being natural. We start thinking too much, and we start becoming divorced from our settings a little bit, and from our vulnerability and our heart. And some of our confidence and inner strength and love and compassion.

That world that we create is just a whole fucking hallucination. It's a whole matrix of ideas and attributes that we attach to things (attributes are just properties that we attach to things, like or dislike, strong or weak, competent or not, funny or not). Attributes themselves are just attributes; they're not actual things in and of their own. That's what Saran Wrap kind of is.

So this world of thinking creates a story that we're in, that we're constantly managing and playing, almost as characters in stories. And that's not really living. That's just playing a fucking role.

Dude, you don't have to pump yourself up to be with somebody or talk to them or interact with them authentically. You just relax a little bit, and you don't have to think about it so much at all. Just do it without thinking about it. Just do the thing; don't think about how you're doing it. Don't think about what the point is. Don't think about if you're good enough. Don't think about what's going to happen. Just do it, and you'll think as you do it. You're talking to somebody? You're just talking to somebody. That is how you get out of your anxiety. It's not through pumping up. It's through doing it. Don't think about it, don't be afraid of it. Just do it. I love that.

That's what "believe in yourself 100%" means. It means you're just being yourself. That's don't know mind. You're just doing it, or you're just breathing. You're not thinking, or you're not trying to control it. That's real don't know mind. That's what you have to practice all the time.
