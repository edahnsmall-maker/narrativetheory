Normally I make my pitch about why you should go on a retreat, but this time I'm going to do the exact same thing but slightly more fun. Without further ado, I present the 5 phases of going on a retreat.

1. EXCITEMENT: "Oooh, sounds interesting." This is the first phase when someone mentions the retreat and you think: wow that sounds amazing! I would love to go!
2. RESENTMENT: "Ehhhh." The "ehhhh" stage is when you start thinking about what else you want to do on Saturday, and usually involves some kind of activity centered around food, friends, sleep, or doomscrolling. The narrative begins to shift. You think about everything that could go wrong. Your body will hurt, your mind will be a mess, and you're going to miss out on brunch.
3. COMPROMISE: "Alright, fine." You start negotiating with yourself. Yeah, you don't really want to go, but you know it'll be good for you. "Screw it, I'll go. At least I won't suffer alone."
4. SURPRISE: "This isn't so bad." You made it to the retreat and you're actually sitting. It's not as bad as you thought. You even had 8 seconds where you weren't thinking about that brunch.
5. EMBRACE: "OMG I NEED TO DO THIS EVERY MONTH, WHY AM I SO HAPPY." The final stage that kicks in between 0-2 days after the retreat where you realize you're naturally happy, calm, and open. You wonder why you don't meditate more often. Maybe I should become a monk, you say to yourself.
