I wish I read more poetry.

I wonder if I'd write better then. Probably.

Now my poetry is more like a diary entry.

Sometimes I can feel the poetry, like a force of nature, or a wind, or a spirit, move through me.

Making my hands dance on the desktop computer. Or the fingers on the phone. And sometimes the pen on the paper.

But most of the time I just try and find my voice, by trying on different voices.

Each with a different rhythm in his writing, and with a different melody in his words.

My favorite poetry is the stuff that has no pretention.

Reading can give you pretensions, but also get rid of them.
