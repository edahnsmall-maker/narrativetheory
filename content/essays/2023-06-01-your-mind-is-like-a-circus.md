I'm going to share a little story. When I finished school back in 2007 I moved back to Los Angeles. I knew I wanted to study Zen, so I googled a few places and a Zen center popped up. Within the first few visits we had interviews which I understood to be opportunities to talk to a Zen teacher. To me this was a chance to ask all my burning philosophical and personal questions... how do I overcome anxiety? how do I meditate correctly? what is the meaning of anything? is Enlightenment what I think it is?

I met with the teacher, Paul Park, and jumped right in. I fired off at least 5 questions, then gave context and elaborated on what I thought the answers were, and then asked new questions, all within about a minute. It was half therapy, half Zen interview. Paul looked at me and started laughing and said "your mind is like a circus!" and I started laughing too. I knew I found a home.

I can tell you that my mind is still a circus, but it's a bit more organized.
