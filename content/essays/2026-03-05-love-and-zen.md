Lately a lot of people I know have been getting pregnant and dealing with the ~~nuclear fallout~~ consequences of their decision. It's been fascinating watching the various couples navigate through the challenges of pregnancy while also trying to maintain their relationships. It's like I'm watching my own personal dating show. Some couples do well while others struggle. Why?

Surprisingly it's not money or resources or time. Or even exhaustion. It comes down to how much time they spend in their stories vs. reality.

The stories follow the same patterns every time: "they're the problem" or "they're too different" or "they're not enough for me." The stories can get very elaborate, but they all seem to come down to that. The more emotionally charged, the harder it is to drop the story.

Dropping the story is what we do in meditation. In fact I'd argue it's really all we do. We find a thing to focus on and slowly emerge out of the story…the drama of life…the delusions that we vow to "cut through". As we emerge from story, we notice a few things:

1. Kindness is already there
2. We're strong, strong enough to say we're sorry or just stop litigating our point
3. We have patience and openness
4. We perceive the delusion and how it runs our perception
5. We feel our way into acting correctly
