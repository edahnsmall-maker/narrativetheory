Thinking is powered by resistance. We're constantly looking to improve, modify, find what's missing and correct it. Even when we get something, it's just a matter of time until we habituate and raise the bar.

This never-ending game is captured in the first noble truth: life is suffering. Life is just life, but our thinking is always creating friction with it. It's always looking to modify it. And when the mind is clogged with thinking and aspirations, it can't settle. It can't see that you already have what you need. It can't see the underlying, fundamental truth of experience and identity. This isn't mysticism or a belief you can pop into your head. This is the reality of every moment you have to see for yourself. No teacher, no guru, not even a meme can give it to you.

Be quiet. Be still. See what's here already. Let it be what it is. Don't touch it. See what happens when your thinking, expectations, judgments, hopes and thoughts about what already is right now all wind down. What's left? What is that?
