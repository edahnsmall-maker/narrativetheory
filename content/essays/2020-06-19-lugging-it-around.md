I want you to not try to make something happen tonight.

I want you to recognize that you came in already wanting a lot of things. Your mind is full, and not because of your fault. Because there's so much to keep track of these days. Things to avoid: shame, social humiliation, pain and fear. We fear the fear! Not wanting to look weird.

But we are all weird.

But we have the illusion that it's just us, and everyone else is the same. It's a thought we have stirred up in us. It might be social media, it might be universal. Maybe someone can tell me.

And because of that, generally our minds are busy. They're in management mode. And that management mode gets paired with other modes called for in the moment: patterns of behaviors, thinking, talking, moving and body stuff, and they can be in different flavors based on what we have going on.

When you come in to meditation we're already thinking there's a task. Because we're always thinking about tasks. What we have to do.

And in meditation, there's tasks, right? This is how you meditate. And you have to do this.

And we get so stressed out, quietly.

We don't even realize all those thoughts are active. We are in a mode of goal achievement, done in service of not trying to look weird. That self-management mode is almost always active.

Well, guess what. This is not one of those things.

This is what happens when you take a break from the management and thinking. You can take a break from it. You don't realize you're actually pushing it. You're lugging it around.

Now get intimate with the breath. Don't try and change it. Just let it go. And be intimate with it. That's focus.
