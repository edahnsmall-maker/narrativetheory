Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to identify a relationship in your life that could use a little Zen. Maybe a colleague, family member, a friend who you've had friction with.

We're going to practice Don't Know with them. All that means is that when you're with them next, try to set aside those automatic opinions about them, that includes who you think they are, why they do the things they do, and the habitual reactions you have to them. (That subtle eye roll, the interruptions, the frustration.)

See if you can just genuinely and gently put it all down. Stay open to having a new experience and learning something new about that person. You don't have to have an amazing, revolutionary interaction with them, but see if you can let a little bit of air into the space between you. See how that feels. See how it affects the interaction. Good luck.
