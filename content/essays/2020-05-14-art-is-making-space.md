Art is simply creating a whole experience. A vibe. You're communicating your vibe; that's why art and empathy go together. You have to be able to feel your own feelings, which arrives at empathy.

Art is making space for an experience that the artist feels, and transmits to the audience. It's like it's projected. Think a DJ, or an artist, or a musician. Think the tea tent at a festival. You make a space and others come in. But you have to hold that mental space, which takes focus, and usually anger or determination or self-connection to avoid hesitation and losing the frame. That frame of mind, whatever it is, is transmitted.

It could be loving, it could be anger, it could be grooving with no care in the world (that's what we normally call art, maybe). They're all art. An easier-to-agree-upon definition is that it's a flow state that's transferred, or a personal state the artist has. It's not subjective in the sense that it's open to interpretation. It does require a subjective (personal) experience though. When the artist and the audience connect, the art communication process is complete.

The components: the artist, the art state (the frame), the observer, the transmission, and the shared experience that arises out of the transmission.
