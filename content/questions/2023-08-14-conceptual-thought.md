Conceptual thought is when your mind starts wandering and telling you a story about something, usually with words and images. When you try to just sit still, eventually something pops into your mind...a conversation with a friend or family member or boss you imagine having, or maybe it's something about what you have to do, or even thinking about what meditation is and whether you're doing it right. All these are conceptual thoughts.

It's very hard to force yourself to stop thinking because that can itself be a form of thinking. It's subtle sometimes, but you can really tell by how relaxed you are vs. how much you're straining to keep your mind a certain way. Instead, you create an environment for conceptual thinking to kind of melt. As you can guess by what I just wrote, it's not through straining and trying to get somewhere. It's more by sitting still and gently bringing your mind to what's happening around you (including your breathing). When conceptual thinking happens you don't have to get upset like you failed. You can notice it then come back to what's happening and your breathing. Again and again.
