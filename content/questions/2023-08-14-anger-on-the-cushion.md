Just today I was sitting for meditation and pretty enraged over my personal story (work shit, respect shit, control shit). I noticed my desire to overcome it or get rid of it. Then I realized that I didn't really want to do that. I just wanted to sit with it and not change it. Then it transformed (not immediately) into introspection, reflection, honesty, vulnerability.

For me, it's all about not resisting but also not indulging. It's something in the middle of both.
