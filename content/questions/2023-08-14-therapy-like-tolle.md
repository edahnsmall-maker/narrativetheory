I'm a therapist and have not found anything. The closest thing is actually a Buddhist community and teacher, and many happen to be therapists too. If you could find a therapist who has any strong practice in Zen, Buddhism, or its analogues, you'll be closer than anything in my opinion.

There are mindfulness-based versions of lots of modern evidence-based therapies, like mCBT, but they wouldn't go down to the level you're looking for, nor should they. If you really want to make some sense of things, a Buddhist teacher would be your best bet. Many offer dokusan ("interviews") that can be light therapy sessions.
