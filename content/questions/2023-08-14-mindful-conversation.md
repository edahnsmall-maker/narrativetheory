---
title: Can trying to be mindful make conversation harder?
date: 2023-08-14
tags: relationships, everyday life, meditation
summary: "Trying too hard to be mindful or spiritual can make you \"tight\" and kind of wound up."
from: Reddit comments Q&A.txt, lines 98 and 105 (two comments on the post "Ego-less socializing"), joined
---

When I feel present I don't find it hard to talk nor do I find it hard to keep things flowing. I'm not trying to be mindful in those moments really.

Trying too hard to be mindful or spiritual can make you "tight" and kind of wound up, where conversation can seem mundane and unspiritual or irrelevant. But it's just life.

When I feel present, I usually am not trying to be present. It becomes a little effortless and connecting becomes natural and easy. One aspect of being mindful is that you aren't ever *thinking* about being mindful.
