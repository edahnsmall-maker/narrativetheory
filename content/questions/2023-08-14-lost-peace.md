---
title: How do I get back the peace I used to have?
date: 2023-08-14
tags: meditation, the mind
summary: The solution is not to try to get to peace, but to make peace with how things are right now.
from: Reddit comments Q&A.txt, line 6 (post: "Lost in spiritual seeking")
---

You're trying to get somewhere, a place of peace you had in the past, and in the process, are actually cultivating negative rejection of this moment and judgement of it too.

When you had that peace, you weren't looking to get somewhere, you just were okay with how things were, regardless of *what* they were. So the solution is not to try to get to peace, but to make peace with how things are right now in the most genuine way you can, and to the extent you can. That's all.
