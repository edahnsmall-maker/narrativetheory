The big secret is that you don't actually go from point A to point B in being present. That's where everyone gets tripped up, including me very often when I meditate. Instead you're tuning in to where you *already* are. What's it like when you just sit down? Before you try to "meditate"? Don't try to use words to describe your state of mind, just watch what's happening. Watch what your body's like, and what the experience as a whole is like. That is it. There's nothing more to it.

If it starts to change, ease up, become more tense, etc., observe that too. Stay with whatever comes up. Even if thoughts come up, see them as part of the whole experience.
