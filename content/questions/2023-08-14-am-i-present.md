Any answer is really going to give you an idea, and if you look at your experience trying to measure it against this idea, you're only going to be thinking. The evaluation of whether you are being present is a thought you are in, not really presence. It's almost like you're swimming in the thought trying to find your way out.

Being present can be described lots of different ways. I think of it as the remainder of your experience when you aren't thinking...(or evaluating, right? see above). You're just there, simply. Just whenever you kinda are. You aren't doing anything specific, you're just being. Whatever is happening outside, in your body, in your experience, is just lightly experienced without being thought about and reflected upon and elaborated upon.
