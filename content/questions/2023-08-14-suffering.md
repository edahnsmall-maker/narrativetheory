The way I see it is that life is constantly falling apart and disappointing you. The key is "you." It's not inherently violent, but from your vantage point, it looks like it's at odds with you. It's dismantling the body, the mind, and everything you want to hold onto. That's the first and second noble truth.

The last part is that if you look really deeply into the nature of your existence, which is to say, consciousness, you can start to see that the things you hold onto emanate from a need to expand and preserve your sense of self. And more importantly, that that sense of self is not an actual thing. It's an artifact of language and habit, but it's not an actual thing. Once the self falls apart the desires that it conjures also fall apart and the suffering becomes a lot less personal and loses its sting.
