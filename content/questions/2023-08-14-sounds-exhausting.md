I struggled with this too. I brought it up to a teacher and rather than giving me an answer he said "wow, sounds exhausting." We both kinda laughed and the next day (this is on retreat) the words echoed in my head, which made me wonder "what the hell am I trying to do here? and why?"

It made me realize that I'm always trying to get somewhere because I always think there's something wrong with the way things already are. But there really, truly isn't. There's nothing wrong with my meditation, nothing wrong with this moment, and nothing wrong with me.

At that point I stopped trying to have any real solid technique and my thoughts just floated on by. I stopped trying to *move* my attention at all because it didn't matter: if there's nothing wrong with it, there's no point changing it. It all moves while "you" stay steady. And it's all groovy.
