I love *Dropping Ashes on the Buddha*. But it might be confusing. Seung Sahn (my teacher coincidentally! not directly tho) like other Zen Masters would teach Zen through showing you what to do. In Korean Zen, Zen mind is called "Don't-Know Mind." It's the mind you get when you enter a state of deep not knowing, which is kind of like confusion, but maybe even beyond that.

His answers to students often point out truths but also try to induce don't-know mind, so as a reader looking for understanding, you may get frustrated, unless you realize that that's the exact point.
