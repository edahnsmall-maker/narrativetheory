Stay aware of the things that don't "look like" presence. That's how you turn them into presence too. The content doesn't matter as much as the relationship to the content.

You can meet any experience with presence and turn it into presence. I personally spent a lot of time (and still do) looking at my experience and thinking "this isn't it." For example, when my mind is busy, or when I don't feel peaceful, or when I feel kind of empty and non-intimate.
