It takes some time. But one thing I would point out is that having an expectation that things should change can undermine the full acceptance of them. Full acceptance kinda means opening up and letting yourself and your body be where it is and being okay with that. Okay, okay, okay... Deeper and deeper... Okay, okay, okay...

Try really settling into that (that being your current state) without waiting for *that* to change and see how that goes. You might feel something different than what you're expecting to feel.
