---
title: How do I let go of an intense attachment to someone?
date: 2023-08-14
tags: relationships, desire
summary: Don't try to be present. Don't try to figure out your feelings. Just drop it and wait.
from: Reddit comments Q&A.txt, line 100 (post: "Extreme attatchment to a person")

It takes time and not thinking. Don't try to be present. Don't try to figure out your feelings. Just drop it and wait. Get your mind on other things. Eventually you'll be able to see things clearly and see what's really up. Then you'll be able to detach fully.
