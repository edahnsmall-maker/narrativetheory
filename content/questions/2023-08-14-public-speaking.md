I'll tell you what I do, because I also get nervous when I publicly speak.

Try to regulate your energy *before* you speak. Don't try to suppress your feelings. Just focus on what feelings you have and let them breathe. Expose them to air and let them cool out a bit. I take about 5-15 minutes and it makes a difference even if I'm not actually sure it made a difference before I speak.
