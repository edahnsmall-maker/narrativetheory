from: Reddit comments Q&A.txt, line 96 (post: "One of the indicators of the ego in me is how sad I get when I get rejected...")

Sometimes it can be healthy to have a strong ego that balances out the shame and guilt and making everything personal. You (your mind) are choosing a narrative that crushes you. But is it true?

You don't know. So you might as well choose the narrative that works to your favor or just wholeheartedly accept that you really don't know. Using that spirit or attitude (of self protection, self advocacy) you can find ways to get around a lot of self-harassment... Someone doesn't like you? Maybe they're shallow. Or just not for you. Maybe they've got their own issues... Point is, it doesn't have to be about you. And it doesn't have to be about you being worthless.

Defend yourself and realize that those thoughts--the ones that are the most self-defeating--are just thoughts... They're not truth. They're not laws. Just ideas. Ideas are wrong all the time.
