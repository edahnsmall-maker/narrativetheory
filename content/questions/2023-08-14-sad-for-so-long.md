When you're really used to being someone (an identity you've developed for a long time) it's hard to let go sometimes. People also have expectations about your behavior that keeps those patterns in place. And then there are certain payoffs that you get from your habits and patterns--things like attention, anger, sadness, misery, and the feeling of strength that can sometimes follow those emotions. They strengthen your sense of identity, or ego.

Given that, how do you drop it? Well, you consider not letting it control all your reactions. You run an experiment where you just don't react the way you normally would. You can even try and do the opposite: instead of getting angry, act nice! It'll seem weird at first, but it'll help you break your patterns.
