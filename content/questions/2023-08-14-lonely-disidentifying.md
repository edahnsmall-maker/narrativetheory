In my experience, disidentifying happens almost by chance rather than planning. Eckhart calls it grace. But once that happens, silence pervades. Part of letting go (or disidentifying) is not evaluating or judging your current experience. I would think that if you feel BOTHERED BY loneliness, that's because there's still evaluation going on and you haven't let go of trying to control and steer your experience. The arising of a lonely feeling is totally okay and maybe even normal. Just be patient with it and watch it evolve.

For me, my practice helps me feel more like me in some ways. I'm less driven by fear and more authentic. That's ok. The disidentification just means I'm not only my thoughts... My identity is something more fundamental. (Even saying "MY identity" is already a mistake and implies this ego thing, but it's easier to talk this way.)
