date: 2023-08-14

The purpose of self-realization isn't to avoid life... It's to free yourself from the ignorance (misconception) that actually leads you *away* from this moment. By realizing you are not your mind, and indeed are nothing permanent (no self) you can get out of your head and live in this moment more fully. It's not a philosophy of avoidance, even if some distort it. Don't be like them!
