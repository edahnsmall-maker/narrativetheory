First remember that a diagnosis is not a real "thing." It's a name some people have assigned to a pattern of behavior, thinking, and feeling. That pattern is not fixed or permanent. In fact, it's changing all the time. And if it's changing all the time it can't be "you." It's just something that passes in and out of consciousness.

You can also keep in mind that diagnoses change all the time and are a relatively new thing in psychiatry. So don't invest too much into it. Take care of yourself and heal as you need. Stay open to change and surprise.

Good luck friend. You are much more than a diagnosis and much more than your "flaws." Remember that and peg your self worth onto your whole self.
