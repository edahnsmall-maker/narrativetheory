Everybody has ambient thinking. And if you have a personality quirk that sometimes comes out, paranoia, anger, it gets ambiently activated all the time. For the paranoia it's looking at your state and thinking something is wrong. Something missing. Not confident enough.

Stopping that chase is what we're meant to be about. Zen Masters don't have anything you don't have. They just have less. They don't have the paranoia. They're not always trying to fix something. You don't have to monitor all the time, dude.
