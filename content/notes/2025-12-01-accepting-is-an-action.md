The issue of straining is important. Not rejecting implies accepting, and accepting implies an action, and an action is a movement. Not trying to make peace, not trying to have an experience, not focusing that hard. Focusing is thinking: evaluating, holding, measuring. The whole thing is acting. This is about being.
