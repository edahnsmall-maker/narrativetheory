It's not the facts. It's all the attributions we layer onto the facts: the history, the context, that turn into denser and denser evaluations that eventually start to become automatically activated whenever you can "feel" the person in the room. It's not just them. It's what you've developed about them. They can be in a great mood but you won't feel it.
