Western psychology teaches us to try and overcome, dominate, and master our experience. But what would happen if we instead allowed it to be and took a deep breath to quietly collect ourselves? What would happen if we changed our relationship to our unwanted experience, and instead of going to war with it, decided to say hello, be nice, and maybe even be friends one day?
