We make meditation into a mental game when it's just not. It's just being bored. And actually the peace and quiet is enjoyable. When you're quiet you're just here. It feels pleasant sometimes but other times it's just numb, but usually it's intimate.

I habitually think this is not it when this is actually always it. It just takes different shapes.

Never look away. This is always it.
