Being yourself and not caring is the wonderful thing. You're not advertising, you're not putting on an ad. People put on ads. Those personal ads. People are marketing themselves and showing off and demonstrating something. That belongs in narrative theory. People are always writing their own ads, always worried, trying to get something, trying to maintain something, or just trying to keep their mind operating. They don't really know how to relax well.
