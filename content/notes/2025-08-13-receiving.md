When you try and approach meditation as a task that you're going to monitor and figure out from your head, almost like a mastermind, you're not meditating, because meditation happens before that. It's just a listening process. I don't like the word observing. Observing, for me, involves mental stuff. It's receiving. Receiving is a much better word, without analysis and calculation.

Meditation is not something you can do, because there's nothing to do, and that is what defines it.
