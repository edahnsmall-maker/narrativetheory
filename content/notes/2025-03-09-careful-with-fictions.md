There are real fictions in life: good and bad, territory, property, right and wrong. Those are all fictions. And people don't really realize that. The truth is, you actually have to be really careful introducing that to people, because if they believe it, they just go into being assholes. And while maybe right and wrong is hallucinated, when you realize it's hallucinated, you also realize, oh, everything's happening, and it's significant. There's a fucking reality going on.
