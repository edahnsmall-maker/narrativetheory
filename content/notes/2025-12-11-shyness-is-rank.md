I think I know what shyness is. It's an automatic assumption about rank. Because we construct these sort of social worlds, it's very important for us to know who's who in the social kingdom, and rank is our way of managing that. And when you have a ranking system that ranks everybody above you, that's imposter syndrome. And you better believe that if it's in a small part of life, it's in a big part of life.

That's really what shyness is. It's an automatic ranking of an other as superior. It's a belief that they have something that you don't, and everything filters into that perception.
