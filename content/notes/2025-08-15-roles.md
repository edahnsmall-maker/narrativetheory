People assume roles because they're uncomfortable with improvisation. They're not comfortable not knowing what their role is. Roles give them an identity. They make people predictable. There's security in performing a role.

Sometimes one role generates a counter-role. A dominant role can create a submissive role in another person, and vice versa.
