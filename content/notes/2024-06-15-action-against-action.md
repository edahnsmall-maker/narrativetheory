What I find a lot of my experience is, is actioning against action. The action I'm taking is trying to change my mood or my experience because I see that it's not clear. The second action is the action that's already there: the mental resistance or ideas or content that's driving a mood or experience that I'm trying to change. There's already some action, and now I'm adding more action. The solution is to just see how things are, which is always where meditation leads.
