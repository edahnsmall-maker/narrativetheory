When we first start to practice we're looking for something for ourselves, maybe escape, or a new skill or overcoming a problem. But soon our experience teaches us that this searching is itself part of the problem, part of what keeps the mind activated and agitated. But humans are clever! So we pivot and commit to eliminate all desires! A great plan! But this new plan then just turns into another desire, a goal we keep in the back of our minds as we sit to meditate.
