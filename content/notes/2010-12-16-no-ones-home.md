What many of us do when we experience anxiety is try and cover it up because we perceive it as being socially or spiritually unacceptable. By trying to cover things up, we end up disconnecting from ourselves and our bodies and we're unable to experience intimacy with others. It's like people coming to visit you, but finding that no one's home.
