Buddhist cosmology separates the world of rebirth and existence (samsara) into 6 realms: gods, demi-gods, humans, animals, hungry ghosts, and hells. You can think of these realms not just as physical realms that we travel to upon death, but mental "places" we travel to throughout the course of our day. Sometimes we feel like demi-gods, other times like insatiable hungry ghosts.
