It's so weird how we Americans walk around with this defective view of ourselves that always needs work and improvement. Like we're constantly cultivating, and that's partly why we don't grow as much as we can. We're not actually broken! We're not defective. We're just following our nature. We are a part of nature. Nature flows through us. How can it be wrong? It doesn't have valence.
