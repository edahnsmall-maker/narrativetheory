I think art is humans going beyond thinking. Not always, but it often has a feeling of release and acceptance. Like singing about life, maybe. But the performance of art needs to be with a clear mind too. Maybe not no thought. Maybe just no self-consciousness. One mind, not no mind. No *extra* mind.
