There's the injury, like a scar, and then there's all the shit around it: the story. When you fill that story, "I'm in grief," "I can't trust people," that's just narration. It's not actually life. You don't have to listen to all your thoughts. There's no point in making the centerpiece of your life your suffering. That's just fetishizing it. Maybe the only thing to make a centerpiece is your practice.
