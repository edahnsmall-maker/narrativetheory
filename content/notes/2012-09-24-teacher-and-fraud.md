A lot of spiritual frauds will tell you that they can do something to fix you or improve you, but that's all bullshit. That's the real difference between a genuine teacher and a fraud. A fraud makes money by suggesting or implying that you are damaged and in need of repair. A real spiritual teacher teaches you that you don't have anything wrong with you to begin with. That single fundamental insight makes all the difference.
