To really connect you have to really be centered. Centered means your energy isn't in a big ball but it's relaxed and smooth and able to resonate with another person's energy without having to perform, or without having a goal, or without trying to get something out of it. A lot of interactions secretly have goals that we're trying to extract from other people.
