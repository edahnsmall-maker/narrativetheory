People settle into conversational and emotional, psychological patterns, or dances. Once it's established, it takes conscious effort to break. Especially abusive or hostile dynamics. The victim and resentor position is very sticky in particular. People enjoy it on different levels. The power trip, the division of self and other, the tragedy, the way it simplifies complex situations. The rejection of other. All incentives.
