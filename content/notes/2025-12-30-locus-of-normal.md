The way you construct reality around somebody is your rank. Rank is basically: do you feel threatened by the person? Most people are in your zone, especially friends. In your zone, you don't react. As you move out of the zone, you start to react. If it's too high, you fear them and go into alert mode.

In alert mode, everything becomes monitoring your own progress with the assumption that you're failing. Your own activity is no longer the locus of normal. Somebody else becomes the center. That's the core of confidence loss. It's actually very big.

So what can you do? Just relax. Let your mind go and see how your outcomes change.
