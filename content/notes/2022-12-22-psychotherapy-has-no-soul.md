Psychotherapy has no soul. No guide. As a discipline we don't have goals and a worldview that is valuable to clients. Therapists should be wise and they should know how to guide without getting into any kind of dogma. It needs to be real.

Talking about trauma can help explain origins, but I don't think it can help change patterns.
