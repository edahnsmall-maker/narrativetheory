You know that moment when you're tired of fighting and your voice shifts? Your eyes and face become relaxed. Your voice becomes relaxed. Your movements slow down. Your focus sharpens. You aren't an asshole, but are 0% a pushover. You aren't needy and aren't making a mess in your life because you're needy for something. That is honesty. That is meditation. They're the same thing.
