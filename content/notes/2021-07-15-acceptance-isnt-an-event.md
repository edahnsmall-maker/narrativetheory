Acceptance isn't an event. You don't cry. You don't ever force yourself to see something a certain way. But you come to an understanding that things don't really change. You just make the best move you can in every moment. But you accept the way things are, rather than overlay your expectations, which immediately lead to judgments (favorable or unfavorable) and anger or disappointment (or elation). And conflict.
