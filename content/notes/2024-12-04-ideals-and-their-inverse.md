All judgment implies ideals. Ideals are part of the scoring system. Their inverse is also part of it: worst-case scenarios. The person who aspires to be rich is also the person who denigrates being poor.

It's almost inverted, though. The immediate mood, the energy-driven feeling, conjures the scoring system to support it. Feelings can exist without scoring system articulations (see: dogs).
