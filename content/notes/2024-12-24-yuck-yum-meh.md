Things have a meaning to us. The meaning has a meaning. At the lowest level we just have a visceral reaction of YUCK or YUM or MEH. Then we can start to invent reasons we like or don't like something, but the actual reasons might exist, just not be brought to the forefront of awareness. When a dog doesn't like another dog, it's not necessarily because it has conscious verbal reasons. It just doesn't like the vibe and messages it's getting from another dog.
