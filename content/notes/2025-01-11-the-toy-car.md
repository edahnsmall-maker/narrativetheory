Meditation could be thought of as having two parts: the alignment, where you tune into the present moment, sort of like rotating a toy car to the right angle, then the process of really letting go, mixed with an inquiry about the self, or at least a curiosity. That's the car traveling. And how ironic that the only traveling we do in meditation is actually moving closer to stopping any traveling at all.
