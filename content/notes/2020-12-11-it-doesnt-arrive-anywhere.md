A relationship doesn't actually have a script. It's all improvised, based on an "authentic," which is to say a real, actual, 1:1 perception of a situation (including the other, yourself, your surroundings), and then just acting. Without any real big huge goal. Or without it trying to arrive somewhere. That's the big thing. It doesn't arrive anywhere. That's the big big thing you arrive at. Usually through friendship. But getting to know someone is friendship, if you're able to put down your expectations.
