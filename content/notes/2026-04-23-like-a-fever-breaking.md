There's a waking up that happens every time we start to pay more attention to what's going on in life than what's going on in our personal story. Something clicks, like a fever breaking or waking up from a nightmare. All the energy we put into managing life through our thinking suddenly seems absurd and pointless. What were we even worried about? What were we doing?
