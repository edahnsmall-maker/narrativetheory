At the gym, I realized that monitoring whether you're self-monitoring is just another form of monitoring. Another version of checking, worrying, occupying the mind. Versus just jumping in, regardless of whether there's a little anxiety. Not monitoring it. Just doing the thing. Having as much fun as you can. And if you don't feel it, you don't feel it. You don't have to feel everything.

That felt freeing.
