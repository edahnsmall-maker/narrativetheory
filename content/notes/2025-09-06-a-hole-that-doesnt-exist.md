Zen is about calming your mind and looking at yourself and your nature to see that the only material you ever have is experience, and it's always changing. Those are the skandhas, and that's emptiness. It's immediate release from suffering, because suffering comes from wanting and having goals and objectives, strategists who do campaigns. That's activity that comes from the void. It's trying to fill a hole that doesn't exist.
