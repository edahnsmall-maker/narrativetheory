I realize how crazy I am all the time, because for a few minutes, I get a little bit of sanity.

The boundary between crazy and non-crazy isn't that clear. We call psychosis "crazy," but pre-psychosis is just delusion. It's being in your head. And we don't really have a word for that; we just call it "sane." But it's not sane. How many people fit that definition and are just miserable, constantly making problems, unable to sit still, always chasing something, or living in violent mental worlds?
