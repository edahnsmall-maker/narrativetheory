I have a habit of thinking of failure. It might not really be my fault. Letting go of guilt is actually a change in perception and understanding of actual causation. Causes work on the basis of inertia. They keep having effects with everything. We still don't understand that being. This. We are the inertial effect of the first cause.

We don't cause our problems. We don't have to feel ashamed of them. Don't neglect this in therapy.
