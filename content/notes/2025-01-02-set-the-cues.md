I think I sometimes wait to take social cues from people, thinking there is a social right, and there is no social right. The most confident person sets the rules, and there are no real rules to begin with. You just sort of set them. That's why cult leaders get into power and start religions and shit, because they believe in their setting, their way of doing things.

But why don't I just set the cues? Why don't I just set the pace and the style and whatever it is? It doesn't have to be with force. It could just be proposed and introduced.
