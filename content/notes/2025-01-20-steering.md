When I'm in this micromanaging mode, I feel like it's checking, holding, making, but also steering. Steering is trying to produce an experience. When you really give up steering, you're just kind of here. It's not necessarily like everything is amazing. It's just sitting here. It's just quiet, and you're not thinking, and you're not checking, and you're not holding things, and you're not making, and you're not getting wrapped up in your thought. You're just going back to zero, back to reset.
