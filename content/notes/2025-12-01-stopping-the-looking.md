Everyone starts the quest trying to find a solution. But Zen is the stopping of the looking. Buddha came up with the method for doing that, but Zen invented new techniques to jump to enlightenment. It's just jhana practice. That's all it is. It's a piece of Buddhism that got engaged to Taoism.

Zen eventually points to the conclusion that we have to stop the search as part of the search.
