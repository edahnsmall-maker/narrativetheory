Accepting your thinking doesn't mean BELIEVING THE CONTENT of your thinking. It just means accepting that you are having those thoughts. It's like a salesman showing up in your living room. You don't have to buy what he's selling you. You're just accepting that he's in there trying to sell you something. Eventually he will realize you're not buying the product and will leave.
