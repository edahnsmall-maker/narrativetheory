Human life is I want! Even to direct yourself toward extinguishing desires is a want or desire. Strictly speaking, desire or even preference is not the problem. Clinging and self-centered craving is really the core of the issue. Why do I want, and for whom? When you're hungry, eat; when someone else is hungry, give them some food.
