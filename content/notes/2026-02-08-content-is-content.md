Maybe it's not the experience that changes. It's the reaction to the experience. It's not the content that needs to change. Content is content. But when you relate to content differently, it stops being content. Without shame. Without making shame. With courage. Without getting lost in the story.

This is where psychotherapy sometimes goes wrong. It asks you to make stories out of things that maybe shouldn't be stories.
